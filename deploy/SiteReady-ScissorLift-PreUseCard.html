1 Before you elevate — walk around

  • Manuals & decals present and legible
  • Scissor arms & pins undamaged
  • Deck-extension locks work
  • Guardrails solid; entry gate closes & latches
  • Tires & lug nuts sound
  • Horn / alarm works
  • Hydraulic level OK at sight glass — never top up; call service
  • Test both control stations + emergency lowering
  • Inspection current per employer / manufacturer program [VERIFY WITH SME]
  • Ground is firm, flat, level; no holes, slopes, or drop-offs
  • Look up — overhead lines, beams, ducts clear

3 The site rule

SITE RULE · EVERY ELEVATION
Hard hat on. Harness on and clipped to the platform anchor point.
Guardrails are the scissor-lift fall-protection method; tie-off isn't federally required there — it is on boom-type lifts. Harness & hat required under site policy. Clip only to a manufacturer-rated anchor. [VERIFY WITH SME]

4 Remember

1 Firm · Flat · Level 2 Guardrails up, gate closed 3 Don't move elevated unless allowed 4 10 ft from power lines 5 Know both E-Stops 6 Know the descent rescue path 7 Mind the danger zone 8 Stay within rated load
