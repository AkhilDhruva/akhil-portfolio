02 — Design Process

A disciplined, evidence-based process — not decoration.

I run ADDIE as a working loop and pull from the learning science that fits the problem. Every design decision traces back to a learner need and a measurable objective.

A

Analyze

Performance gap, audience, constraints, baseline metric.

D

Design

Objectives, assessment-of-learning, instructional strategy.

D

Develop

Storyboards, prototypes, content, performance support.

I

Implement

Pilot, LMS deploy, champions, change comms.

E

Evaluate

Kirkpatrick L1–4, analytics, iterate.

04 — About

An instructional designer who can build what they design.

I design learning systems for technical and high-stakes environments where unclear training creates real business risk — errors, safety exposure, stalled adoption, and lost productivity.

My edge is that I can design and prototype. I do not just write storyboards and hand them off; I build interactive learning experiences that preserve the instructional intent through branching scenarios, web-based simulations, mobile-first performance support, and accessible interface design.

Across my work, the pattern is translation: converting complex systems into usable learning experiences for the people expected to act on them. Whether the subject is enterprise AI, jobsite safety, or decision-making under pressure, my goal is the same — help teams understand the system, trust the process, and perform with confidence.
